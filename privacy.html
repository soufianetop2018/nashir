Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · Applies to the Nashir desktop application by Syntrix Fikra.

1. Summary

Nashir runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server and no user accounts of its own, and it does not collect, sell or share your data. It talks only to the platforms you connect (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok), from your computer, to publish the videos you choose.

2. Data Nashir accesses

  • Google / YouTube (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): Nashir uploads the videos you queue to your channel with the title, description and tags you set, reads your channel name to show which channel is connected, and reads the privacy status of the videos it uploaded so it can tell you if one became private.
  • Meta / Facebook (permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts): Nashir lists the Pages you manage so you can choose one, publishes the Reels you queue to that Page, and reads the processing status of those Reels.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, optionally video.publish): Nashir reads your display name, sends the videos you queue to your TikTok account (as drafts, or as posts if you choose), and reads their upload status.
  • Google Gemini (optional, off by default): only if you choose “Gemini” as the caption method and paste your own Gemini API key, Nashir sends the video title and the channel name you typed to the Gemini API to write a description and hashtags. This is governed by Google's Gemini API terms; on Gemini's free tier Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Never put private information in a video title if you use this option.
  • Thumbnails (optional): only when you click “Draw pictures”, a short English description of a scene made from the video title is sent to a free image service (AI Horde or Pollinations) to paint a picture. The title text is written on the picture on your computer, and the finished thumbnail goes only to the platforms you publish to.

Nashir does not read your messages, comments, contacts, analytics, other videos or any other data.

3. Where data is stored

Access tokens, the app keys you enter, your queue (video file paths, captions, times) and a log of uploads are saved only in the data folder next to the program on your computer. They are never sent to Syntrix Fikra or to anyone else. Videos are sent only to the platform you selected for them, and video titles to Gemini only if you turned that option on.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, transfer or disclose any user data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

5. Google API Services and YouTube

Nashir's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Nashir uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Nashir's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Deleting your data

Use “Disconnect” in Nashir, delete the data folder, and revoke access on each platform. Details: Data deletion instructions.

7. This website

This website has no cookies, trackers or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

8. Children

Nashir is a tool for content creators and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Contact: syntrix.fikra@gmail.com.

سياسة الخصوصية (بالعربية)

ناشر يعمل بالكامل على حاسوبك، بلا خادم وبلا حسابات خاصة به، ولا يجمع بياناتك ولا يبيعها ولا يشاركها.

  • يوتيوب: يرفع الفيديوهات التي تختارها إلى قناتك، ويقرأ اسم القناة وحالة ظهور الفيديوهات التي رفعها فقط.
  • فيسبوك: يعرض صفحاتك لتختار واحدة، وينشر عليها الريلز التي تختارها، ويقرأ حالة معالجتها.
  • تيك توك: يقرأ اسمك الظاهر، ويرسل فيديوهاتك إلى حسابك كمسودات (أو منشورات إن اخترت)، ويقرأ حالة الرفع.
  • Gemini (اختياري، معطّل افتراضيًا): فقط إن اخترته ووضعت مفتاحك، يُرسل عنوان الفيديو واسم قناتك إلى Gemini لكتابة الوصف والهاشتاغات، وتطبَّق شروط Google. في الخطة المجانية قد تستعمل Google ما يُرسل لتحسين خدماتها، فلا تضع معلومات خاصة في العنوان.
  • الصور المصغّرة (اختياري): فقط عند ضغط «ارسم صورًا» يُرسل وصف قصير بالإنجليزية لمشهد مأخوذ من العنوان إلى خدمة رسم مجانية (AI Horde أو Pollinations). العنوان نفسه يُكتب على الصورة في حاسوبك، والصورة النهائية لا تذهب إلا إلى المنصات التي تنشر عليها.

رموز الدخول والمفاتيح والجدولة والسجل تُحفظ فقط في مجلد data على حاسوبك، ولا تُرسل إلينا ولا إلى أي طرف آخر. استعمال بيانات Google يلتزم بسياسة بيانات مستخدمي Google API بما فيها شروط الاستعمال المحدود، وبربط يوتيوب توافق على شروط خدمة يوتيوب وسياسة خصوصية Google. يمكنك سحب الإذن في أي وقت من صفحة أذونات حسابك في كل منصة.
